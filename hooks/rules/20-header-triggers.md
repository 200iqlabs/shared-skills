## When the header appears — closed list

Emit the orientation header when, and only when, at least one of these holds:

1. It is your first reply in the session.
2. It is your first reply after a resume, or after earlier context was summarised or compacted.
3. The reply concludes a piece of work that took several steps.
4. The reply asks the user a question, delegates a task, or requests a decision.
5. The topic or the stage of work has changed since your previous reply.
6. The user has asked where things stand.

Outside these six, no header.

**Never emit it because the reply feels important.** Your own sense of what matters is the part
that failed before — that judgement is not a trigger. Match the list or omit the header.
